Around Varsoli

A beach day with room to breathe.

Varsoli Beach is known for its long, broad sandy shoreline and tree-lined stretches. Enjoy a sea view, a gentle walk or a quiet moment in the shade. Water activities, swimming suitability and public amenities depend on local conditions and seasonal availability.

Beach-side amenities have included children's play areas, shaded seating and parking. Please check current conditions locally when you visit.
