Welcome to Nisarg

Your peaceful corner of the Konkan coast.

Nisarg Family Guest House is set amidst coconut and areca palm plantations in Varsoli, Alibaug. Whether you are planning a family break or a group getaway, settle into an easy-going atmosphere close to the sea.

Varsoli Beach is known for its broad shoreline, coastal trees and relaxed character. Come to take a walk, enjoy the breeze and make time for the people you love.

  • Family, family-group and corporate-group stays
  • Well-furnished AC and non-AC accommodation
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian meal service available
